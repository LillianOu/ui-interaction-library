12 · Motion Effects

Scroll-driven Vortex Transit

Scroll down and the camera dives through a spinning tunnel. Each headline rushes past you and the next one spirals in from the dark. Scroll faster and the vortex speeds up.

Mouse: scrollTouch: swipeKeyboard: Space / arrows, or Tab to the stop buttons

The tunnel twists harder the deeper you go. Headlines orbit and grow as they reach the camera.

Tune it

speedBase cruise speed is 0.12 depth units a second. Scroll velocity adds up to about 20 times that.
twistSpin goes from 0.35 to 1.1 rad/s with progress. Particles near the camera spin faster.
count720 particles on wide screens and 360 under 600px. They are batched into eight strokes a frame.
coloursRead from the --cyan and --violet tokens, and re-read when the colour scheme changes.
performanceDPR is capped at 2. The loop stops when the scene is off-screen or the tab is hidden, and everything is removed on pagehide.
reduced motionThe tunnel is drawn once, without moving. Panels crossfade in place with the same text, and nothing flies or spins.
