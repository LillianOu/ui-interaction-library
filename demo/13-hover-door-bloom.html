Hover-triggered Door Bloom

滑過開門綻放

Point at a door and it swings open on its hinges. A bloom of petals and pollen opens up behind it and the room inside slides into view. Move away and the door closes again.

Mouse: hover, or click Open / CloseTouch: tap to open or closeKeyboard: Tab opens, Enter toggles, Esc closes

timingOpens in 900 ms with a slight overshoot and closes in 650 ms. The room fades in 180 ms after the door starts to move.
bloomTwo rings of petals (8 and 13) and 40 pollen grains, drawn on a canvas inside the doorway in the cyan and violet tokens.
reachableThe room buttons stay in the tab order. Tabbing to one opens its door, and Esc closes the door and returns focus to its toggle.
reduced motionLeaves fade out instead of swinging and the bloom appears fully open with no animation. The content is the same.
