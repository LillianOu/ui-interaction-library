Scroll-driven Helix Flow

滾動螺旋流動

Scroll down. The section pins, and scroll progress turns a double helix, carries the camera through its depth, and lights each link as it passes. Keep scrolling and it releases the page.

Mouse / trackpad: scrollTouch: swipeKeyboard: Space, Page Down, arrows

02 · Ascent

Through the turn

Scrolling spins the structure and pulls the camera into it. Links light up as the scan passes.

03 · Arrival

Fully linked

Every rung glows. The helix lies down, settles, and lets the page continue.

Exit

The pin releases

Once progress reaches 1 the sticky panel scrolls away with the page, so scrolling is never trapped. Scroll back up to scrub in reverse.

Input
Scroll progress of the bounded section, 0 → 1, eased with a lerp of 0.12 per frame.
Timeline
0–0.5: dolly in from z 17 to 6, tilt and 2 turns. 0.5–1: pull back to z 12, lie the axis flat, all rungs lit.
Render
Canvas 2D projection, 2 × 200 beads with pre-rendered glow sprites, depth sorted. Renders only while on screen.
Reduced motion
The section stops pinning; one static, fully linked helix with the three chapters listed beneath it.
