Orb suspended near the top, bobbing ±7px. Foliage hidden at scale 0.
Transition
1.25s eased drop, 180ms squash, ripple and 90-particle burst, 17 leaves staggered 45ms with overshoot, orb rises to float.

Orb floats above a swaying bloom with slow rising motes. Restart reverses in 0.7s.
