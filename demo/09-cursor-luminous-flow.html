Light that follows intent

A soft volumetric trail, damped by a spring and layered over three depths of parallax.

Move to steer · leave to drift · arrows when focused

Input
pointermove / pointerleave on the scene, normalised to [-1, 1]. Arrow keys nudge the target when the scene has focus.
Motion
Spring stiffness 0.07, damping 0.82. Ribbon of 5 strands over the last 56 positions; particles shed in proportion to speed.
Touch
Follows the finger while dragging sideways (vertical drags still scroll the page), otherwise a slow Lissajous loop.
Reduced motion
One static frame of the same ribbon and copy. No loop, no parallax, no particles.
